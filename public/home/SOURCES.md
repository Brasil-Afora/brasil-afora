# Home page image sources

The hero rotates one pool of twenty-four university photos, shared by both themes (see `src/components/homepage/hero-photos.ts`). The `night/` and `day/` folders below describe the light in the photo — golden hour, blue hour, night and warm interiors, or daylight — not the theme it appears in.

Changes to every file: downloaded from Wikimedia Commons (the 3840px rendition where the original is larger), resized to 2400px wide and re-encoded as JPEG for web delivery; no compositional edits. On the page each is cropped by `object-position` and shown through a CSS color grade, which differs per theme: on the navy ground photos are darkened (daylight ones most of all) and dissolve into the background; on the paper plate they get a light saturation and contrast lift, and the darkest ones are lifted further. See DESIGN.md, The Low-Key Night Rule.
