# Map tiles

`v1/{z}/{x}/{y}.webp` are cut from the repository's `public/map.jpg` (8192×4096, equirectangular night lights; its original source is not recorded in the repo) by `scripts/build-map-tiles.mjs`: Leaflet's EPSG:4326 grid, zoom 0–4, 256px WebP tiles. Zoom 4 is the source's own resolution. No other editing.

The versioned folder is load-bearing. Tiles are cached by URL, so a re-cut must go to a new folder (`v2`, …), and the `/mapa` page must point at it.

The country outlines drawn over the tiles (`src/components/world-map/countries.json`) are generated by `scripts/build-map-countries.mjs` from `src/utils/countries.geo.json`, the Natural Earth-derived world outline set (public domain).
