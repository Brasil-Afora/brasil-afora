# Map tiles

`v1/{z}/{x}/{y}.webp` are cut from the repository's `public/map.jpg` (8192×4096, equirectangular night lights; its original source is not recorded in the repo) by `scripts/build-map-tiles.mjs`: Leaflet's EPSG:4326 grid, zoom 0–4, 256px WebP tiles. Zoom 4 is the source's own resolution. No other editing.

The versioned folder is load-bearing. Tiles are cached by URL, so a re-cut must go to a new folder (`v2`, …), and the `/mapa` page must point at it.

The country outlines drawn over the tiles (`src/components/world-map/countries.json`) are generated by `scripts/build-map-countries.mjs` from `src/utils/countries.geo.json`, the Natural Earth-derived world outline set (public domain).

## Day tiles (light theme)

`day-v1/{z}/{x}/{y}.webp` are cut the same way from `public/map-day.jpg`:

    node scripts/build-map-tiles.mjs public/map-day.jpg public/map-tiles/day-v1

`public/map-day.jpg` (8192×4096, same equirectangular extent as `map.jpg`, so both tile sets share one grid) is built by `scripts/build-map-day.mjs` from NASA's *Blue Marble: Next Generation* July 2004 composite with shaded topography, `world.topo.200407.3x21600x10800.jpg` (21600×10800, sha256 `40a42c45…eda8`; the full URL and hash are in the script). Graded for the paper theme: near-black water repainted a pale sea, land shadows lifted, green pulled toward olive (green means "Verificada" on this site). The script documents the water rules and the measurements behind them.

Credit: NASA Earth Observatory (Blue Marble: Next Generation). NASA imagery is not copyrighted; NASA asks that it be credited, and the /mapa footnote does so in the light theme.
