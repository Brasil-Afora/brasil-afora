# Surface: Home (`/`)

Mode: Persuade. Audience: Brazilian students at every level, arriving cold or from search. Action: search or pick a category, open a verified opportunity, or open the map. Proof on hand: the curated verified set (official links, check date, real deadlines) and the night map. Constraints: verification claims scoped to the curated set; no invented crests, counts or testimonials; existing contact e-mail and submission form kept.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The home is a departure board, not a brochure. It refuses the centered slogan-over-gradient hero with feature cards; the first viewport already lets you search, filter by category, and see which verified opportunities close next.
OWN-WORLD: Deep navy ground (#03111f) under a dusk skyline photo that dissolves into it from the right; one hot amber (#ff9b0f) reserved for the primary action, the active nav and drawn routes; hairline navy rules (#1a3148); blue pills for funding facts, green only for "Verificada". Atkinson Hyperlegible Next for all reading text, Bebas for the wordmark, Caveat for a single handwritten note. Amber lines that draw themselves are the motion grammar.
STORY: A student understands "opportunities for Brazilians, here and abroad" in one line, types a country or program and gets instant matches, sees four verified opportunities ordered by the nearest deadline with days left, learns why the listings can be trusted, and follows arcs from Brazil to the map.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Left 55%: two-line headline (56-60px, bold), one-line promise, a 56px search bar with the amber "Buscar" button, a row of category chips, and a scoped trust line with the check date. Right 45%: the Edinburgh dusk photo, masked into the navy, with the handwritten "Mais conhecimento para um futuro maior." and an amber underline that draws on load. Verified cards start above the fold at 1440x900.
FORM: Owner-pinned mockup (2026-09-21); pinned direction beats the roll, so no concept-seed ran. Code-led (no image generation on this machine); the owner's mockup is the critique reference.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

Signature interaction: live search combobox over the verified set plus both catalogs, keyboard-navigable; secondary motion: amber arcs from São Paulo to each verified destination draw as the map band scrolls into view.

Unresolved: whether the wider catalog will ever be verified (then the trust line can widen).
